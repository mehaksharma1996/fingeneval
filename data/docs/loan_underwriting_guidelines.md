# Loan Underwriting Guidelines

## Borrower Eligibility

Eligible borrowers must provide identity verification, consent for credit review, documented income, and a complete statement of existing obligations. Applicants with unresolved fraud indicators, bankruptcy restrictions, or sanctions screening exceptions are not eligible until the issue is cleared.

## Approval And Decline Logic

Underwriting decisions must consider affordability, collateral coverage, credit history, and policy exceptions. FICO score bands remain controlling for consumer credit decisions: `<580 decline`, `580-669 manual review`, `670+ eligible`. A credit score of `670+ eligible` does not guarantee approval if affordability or documentation standards are not met.

## High DTI Exceptions

DTI thresholds are `43% standard`, `50% maximum with compensating factors`. A borrower above 43% DTI may qualify only through an exception review, and no borrower above 50% DTI may be approved under the standard retail program. Compensating factors must be specific, verified, and retained in the underwriting file.

## Income Verification

Income must be verified through recent pay statements, tax records, employer confirmation, or bank deposit analysis. Self-employment income requires a documented two-year average unless an approved exception is recorded by underwriting management.

## Collateral Requirements

Secured loans require current collateral valuation, lien review, and insurance confirmation where applicable. Collateral strength may support an exception but must not substitute for repayment capacity.

## Escalation And Governance

Files with high DTI, manual FICO review, missing income evidence, or collateral disputes must be escalated to a senior underwriter. All overrides must identify the approving authority, rationale, and borrower-level risk mitigants.
