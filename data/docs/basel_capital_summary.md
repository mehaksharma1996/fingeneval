# Basel Capital Summary

## Basel III Capital Standards

Basel III establishes minimum capital expectations for internationally active banks. The Basel III Tier 1 capital minimum is `6%`, and the well-capitalized threshold is `8%`. Capital ratios are measured against risk-weighted assets.

## Risk-Weighted Assets

Risk-weighted assets reflect credit risk, market risk, and operational risk exposures. Higher-risk assets receive higher weights, increasing required capital and affecting management buffers.

## Credit Risk

Credit risk capital captures the potential for borrower or counterparty default. Retail, corporate, sovereign, and secured exposures may receive different risk weights depending on regulatory treatment and collateral quality.

## Operational Risk

Operational risk capital addresses losses from failed processes, systems, people, external events, fraud, and legal risk. Banks must maintain controls and loss-event tracking to support operational risk management.

## Stress Testing

Capital planning must include stress testing for severe but plausible macroeconomic, market, and operational scenarios. Stress results inform capital buffers, risk appetite, and remediation actions.

## Governance Requirements

Capital adequacy reporting must be reviewed by finance, treasury, risk management, and executive committees. Material capital shortfalls require escalation and documented remediation planning.
