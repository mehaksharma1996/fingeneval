# Credit Risk Policy

## Credit Risk Scoring

The bank uses credit bureau attributes, verified income, debt obligations, and prior repayment behavior to assign a retail credit risk grade. FICO score bands are applied as follows: `<580 decline`, `580-669 manual review`, `670+ eligible`. Credit risk officers must document the score band, bureau date, and any adverse-action reason codes used in the decision.

## Affordability And DTI Thresholds

Debt-to-income affordability is measured using verified monthly debt obligations divided by verified gross monthly income. DTI thresholds are `43% standard`, `50% maximum with compensating factors`. Applications above the standard threshold may not be automatically approved and require documented affordability analysis.

## Manual Review Rules

Manual review is required for any applicant in the `580-669 manual review` FICO band, any borrower with DTI above `43% standard`, and any file with inconsistent income documentation. Reviewers must confirm that repayment capacity is supported by bank statements, pay records, or tax documentation.

## Exception Handling

Exceptions above the standard DTI threshold may be approved only when DTI does not exceed `50% maximum with compensating factors`. Acceptable compensating factors include strong liquid reserves, stable employment history, low loan-to-value collateral, or verified secondary income. Exceptions require second-line credit approval and must not override the `<580 decline` FICO rule.

## Documentation Requirements

Each decision file must include income verification, debt calculation worksheets, credit score evidence, exception rationale, and approval authority. Missing documentation must be escalated to credit risk governance before booking.
