# Customer Complaint Policy

## Complaint Intake

Complaints may be received through branches, call centers, digital channels, regulators, or executive offices. Intake records must capture customer identity, product, issue type, date received, channel, and requested resolution.

## Response Timelines

Standard customer complaints require acknowledgement within five business days and a substantive response within thirty calendar days. Complex complaints may receive an extension when the customer is informed and the reason is documented.

## Complaint Escalation

Complaints involving discrimination, credit denial disputes, regulatory allegations, legal threats, or vulnerable customers must be escalated to complaint governance. Executive complaints require senior management visibility.

## Regulatory Complaints

Regulatory complaints must be logged separately, assigned to a compliance owner, and tracked through closure. Responses must be reviewed for accuracy, completeness, and consistency with policy.

## Root-Cause Analysis

Recurring complaint themes require root-cause analysis, corrective action plans, accountable owners, and follow-up testing. Root-cause results must be shared with product, operations, compliance, and risk committees.

## Documentation Standards

Complaint files must retain customer communications, investigation notes, supporting evidence, response approvals, remediation actions, and closure date. Missing documentation must be escalated before the complaint is closed.
